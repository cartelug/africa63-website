The Kampala press conference brought together media professionals, cultural figures, partners, invited guests and a highly engaged audience. Africa 63's work around the event is presented through a visual record of the stage moments, panel conversations, audience energy and the wider media response.

The gathering drew a packed audience and generated strong public and media attention — a demonstration of the power of cultural events to connect audiences, institutions and regional creative industries. From the main stage to the seated panel, the programme moved between presentation, conversation and celebration.

This engagement positions Africa 63 as an organisation capable of supporting prominent events with professional documentation, strong public visibility, and content suitable for websites, reports, campaigns and institutional communication.

**Africa 63 helps important moments become visible, credible and memorable.**
